PROJECT TITLE

Royalhouse Chapel Worldwide Digital Kingdom Platform

⸻

PROJECT DESCRIPTION

Develop a world-class digital ecosystem that unifies Royalhouse Chapel Worldwide into one secure, scalable, and intelligent technology platform.

This project is much more than a church website.

It is a complete Church Operating System (ChurchOS) designed to support ministry, administration, communication, finance, media, discipleship, leadership, and member engagement across all Royalhouse Chapel branches worldwide.

The platform must be capable of serving millions of users while remaining fast, secure, and easy to manage.

⸻

PROJECT OBJECTIVES

Build one centralized platform that provides:

* Official Royalhouse Chapel Worldwide website
* Member management
* Worldwide branch management
* Leadership management
* Digital giving
* Sermon platform
* Digital bookstore
* Event management
* Prayer platform
* Communication system
* Internal messaging
* Church administration
* Mobile applications
* Analytics and reporting
* AI-ready architecture
* Future multi-church expansion
