ROYALHOUSE CHAPEL WORLDWIDE DIGITAL KINGDOM PLATFORM

PART 3 OF 5

Finance, Giving, Marketplace & Digital Commerce Platform

⸻

OVERVIEW

Develop a secure, enterprise-grade financial and commerce platform fully integrated into the Royalhouse Chapel Worldwide Digital Kingdom Platform.

This system should support church giving, ministry-specific donations, conference registrations, event payments, digital resources, physical products, and detailed financial reporting.

The platform must provide transparency, accountability, security, and flexibility while allowing Headquarters and authorized branch leaders to manage finances according to their assigned permissions.

⸻

ONLINE GIVING CENTER

Create a dedicated “Give” page accessible from both the website and mobile applications.

Design requirements:

* Premium, modern interface
* Clear giving categories
* Secure payment process
* Mobile-friendly
* Recurring giving support
* Donation history
* Instant digital receipts
* Multiple currencies (future-ready for international branches)

SECURITY REQUIREMENTS

Protect all financial information through:

* Secure authentication
* Encrypted payment processing
* Role-based authorization
* Fraud detection support
* Secure transaction logs

Do not store sensitive payment credentials directly in the application.

QUALITY REQUIREMENTS

Every financial workflow should be thoroughly tested.

Ensure:

The Giving & Marketplace Platform should provide a secure, transparent, and professional experience that supports the ministry’s mission while remaining scalable for Royalhouse Chapel Worldwide’s continued global growth.
